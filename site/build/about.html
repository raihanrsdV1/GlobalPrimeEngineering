Our Story

Built on Family Legacy Since 1968

Global Prime Engineering Turning Workshop LLC is a professionally established engineering workshop built on decades of experience and a strong family legacy in the engineering industry.

Our journey began in 1968 with our first family-owned sister concern, Chittagong Engineering Turning Workshop, which laid the foundation for our expertise in precision machining and engineering services.

Today, we specialize in precision turning, machining, fabrication, and maintenance services for industrial, construction, and marine sectors. We combine traditional craftsmanship with modern engineering solutions to deliver quality, durability, and customer satisfaction.

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality, reliable, and cost-effective engineering solutions while ensuring safety, precision, and customer satisfaction in every project.

Our Vision

To become a leading and trusted engineering workshop in the UAE and beyond by providing innovative solutions, maintaining superior quality standards, and continuing our legacy of excellence for future generations.
